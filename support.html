A few useful answers.

When can I download Sculpt?

Sculpt is coming soon to iPhone. We'll add the App Store link to this website when it's available.

What should I include in a support request?

Describe what happened and, if relevant, include your iPhone model and iOS version. Please don't email passwords, payment details, or body photos. If we need more information, we'll ask.

How do I request help with my data?

Email sculpt.app.support@gmail.com with “Privacy request” in the subject. Let us know whether you need help accessing, correcting, or deleting your information. We'll guide you through the appropriate verification steps.

Does deleting my account cancel my subscription?

Account deletion and Apple subscription cancellation are separate. Manage an App Store subscription from Settings on your iPhone: tap your name, then Subscriptions. Contact us if you need help understanding the difference.

Is Sculpt medical advice?

No. Sculpt is designed for general fitness guidance for adults. AI-assisted body composition and calorie insights are estimates and can be inaccurate. They aren't medical measurements, diagnoses, or treatment recommendations.

Questions about launch or availability? You're welcome to get in touch at the same email address.
