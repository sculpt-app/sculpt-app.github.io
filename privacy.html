Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Sculpt is operated by Jacob Lagesse, based in California, USA. This policy covers the Sculpt app, website and related support. For privacy questions or requests, email sculpt.app.support@gmail.com or write to Jacob Lagesse, 655 Bonanza Ct, Sunnyvale, CA 94087, USA.

1. Information we collect

The information handled depends on the features you use:

  • Account information: your account identifier, name, email and authentication information supplied by your sign-in provider.
  • Profile and goals: birth date, gender answer, height, weight, units, activity and training preferences, body goal, focus muscles, target weight and optional target body fat.
  • Photos and analysis: photos you submit, framing and quality results, estimated body-fat ranges, muscle scores, confidence and related explanations, body calibration and calorie guidance.
  • Training and progress: saved routines, exercises and exercise weights, dated body-weight entries, scan history and workout records.
  • Purchases and technical information: subscription status, transaction references, request identifiers, device/app information, and records used for security, troubleshooting and rate limits. Apple processes App Store payments; we do not receive your full payment-card details.
  • Communications: the information you send in support or privacy requests. Please do not email passwords, payment details or body photos.

You provide information directly, through your device and sign-in provider, or through your use of Sculpt. Some information, such as body-composition estimates and recommended routines, is generated from your inputs. These inferences may be health-related information even though they are not medical measurements. See our Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide and secure your account; save and synchronize supported records; provide scans and routines you request; display goals and progress; calculate estimated calorie guidance; administer subscriptions; respond to requests; and meet legal obligations. Your current measurements and target measurements serve different purposes in your personalized guidance.

Sculpt's analysis and routines are automated estimates. They can be inaccurate and are not diagnoses or guarantees. Do not rely on them for medical decisions. You may contact us about inaccurate information or questions about an output.

3. Photos and AI services

Starting a scan, photo-quality check or review sends prepared photos through our Supabase backend to OpenAI for processing. The app crops or masks images, resizes them and removes image metadata before upload. These measures reduce identifying detail but do not make a body photo anonymous.

A scan review may also use your selected body goal, age derived from your birth date, gender answer, height, weight, BMI, activity level, target weight and optional target body fat. Your full birth date is not included in the scan model input.

When you request an AI routine, OpenAI receives selected training days, focus muscles, saved muscle-score context and eligible exercise information. The routine model input does not include your name, email, account identifier, photos or exercise weights.

Photos are temporary inputs rather than a Sculpt photo archive. We do not intentionally save uploaded photos in a Sculpt image-storage bucket. Structured results can be saved to your account. Our API requests disable stored response history, but this does not mean zero retention by providers: security and abuse monitoring, processing records, caching and legal exceptions may apply under OpenAI's API data controls.

You can choose not to submit photos or request AI features. A feature cannot operate without the information necessary for that feature. Where applicable law requires consent, sensitive-data processing and sharing are subject to that consent; accepting the Terms or reading this policy does not replace a separate permission required by law. Contact us to withdraw consent for future processing. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

4. Storage and recipients

Sculpt stores account-scoped information on your device. Signed-in profile answers, scan results, weight history, routines and exercise weights can also be stored in Supabase. Some workout history remains on your device. Deleting local data is different from deleting a cloud account.

We use Supabase for authentication, database and backend services; OpenAI for AI processing; Apple for App Store purchases; RevenueCat for subscription access; and Google for sign-in and support email. Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Providers receive the information needed for their respective services, and may also handle technical, security and legally required records under their own terms and privacy notices.

Authorized personnel and professional advisers may access information when necessary for support, security, legal obligations or privacy requests. We may disclose information to authorities when legally required, or where law permits disclosure to protect people, rights or the service. Any business transfer involving personal information remains subject to applicable privacy law and required notices or permissions.

We do not sell personal information or consumer health data, use body photos or health inferences for advertising, or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Any new purpose requiring consent will require that consent before processing begins.

5. Website visits

The public website has no account form or advertising trackers added by Sculpt. GitHub Pages may process your IP address and ordinary request information to serve and secure the site, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. Emailing support sends your message through your email provider and our Google-hosted mailbox. External links lead to services with their own privacy practices.

6. Retention and account deletion

We keep profile, goal, scan-result, weight and routine records while providing your account, until you request deletion, subject to limited lawful exceptions. Retention of support, operational and security records depends on the purpose, whether an issue is resolved, applicable legal duties, and any need to establish or defend legal claims. Only information needed for those purposes may be retained.

Request account deletion through Profile & settings > Account > Delete account, or email us. Deletion removes or schedules removal of account records across relevant systems. Provider processing and backup cycles can delay complete removal; a successful request does not mean every backup or provider record disappears immediately. Some providers retain records independently, such as Apple's purchase records. Contact us for information about a deletion request or any applicable exception.

You control copies you export or otherwise save outside Sculpt. Deleting the app, clearing local data or deleting your Sculpt account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage subscriptions through your Apple account.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to know or access the information we hold; obtain a copy or portable record; correct information; request deletion; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; and appeal a decision about a request. You may also complain to your local privacy regulator. California residents may have rights under applicable California privacy laws, including protection against unlawful discrimination for exercising their rights.

Send requests to sculpt.app.support@gmail.com, with enough information to identify your Sculpt account and the request. We use proportionate identity verification and handle authorized-agent requests where required. We respond within applicable legal deadlines, explain permitted extensions or refusals, and provide available appeal information. A paid subscription is not required to exercise privacy rights. To appeal, contact the same email with "Privacy appeal" in the subject. An app export may cover only device data; contact us for a broader account-data request.

8. Legal bases and international processing

Where a legal basis is required, we rely on performance of our agreement for necessary account and requested services; legitimate interests in operating and securing the service and responding to requests, balanced against your rights; compliance with legal obligations; and consent where required. Sensitive health-related processing also requires an applicable additional legal condition, including explicit consent where required.

We operate in the United States. Providers may process information in the United States and other countries, whose privacy laws may differ from yours. Where applicable law requires transfer safeguards, transfers are subject to those requirements, such as an applicable adequacy decision or approved contractual safeguards. Contact us for information about recipients, processing locations and safeguards relevant to your information, or to request an available copy of the applicable safeguard.

9. Adults, security and changes

Sculpt is intended for adults aged 18 or older, subject to any higher applicable local age requirement. Do not submit children's information or photos. If you believe a child's information has been provided, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

We use organizational and technical measures to protect information, but no system is completely secure. We address incidents and provide notices where required. We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date identifies the current version; material changes will receive additional notice or consent where required.
